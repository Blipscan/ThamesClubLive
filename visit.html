Your first visit · New London, Connecticut

Come visit
the Thames Club

The best introduction is in person.

A website can show you the rooms. A visit lets you meet the people.

If you are considering membership, we invite you to see our historic clubhouse in downtown New London. Come with your questions and a little curiosity. We will show you around, explain Club life, and introduce you to members.

You do not need to know someone at the Club already.

Request a Visit →Call (959) 264-2733

What to expect

Your first visit is a chance to get acquainted. See the gathering spaces and duckpin bowling lanes, learn about dining and upcoming events, and discuss how membership works.

Let us know if you would like to bring your spouse, partner, or a friend.

Choose a good time

Wednesday lunch can be a convenient opportunity to meet us. Other opportunities depend on the Club calendar.

Please contact us to confirm a suitable time and any meal arrangements before coming.
