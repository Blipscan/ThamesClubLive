# Thames Club flyer and poster branding

Standing direction from Bill, October 25, 2026:

- Use the approved elegant Thames Club calligraphic interlocking monogram in gold on all future flyers and posters.
- Canonical asset: `images/thames-club-monogram-gold.png` in ThamesClubLive. It preserves the shape of the supplied `ThamesClub_Logo_Navy(3).png`, recolored gold (#c9a96e) with transparency.
- Do not substitute anchor pennants, anchors, crests, shields, compass roses, or invented initials for the Club logo.
- Preserve the approved monogram shape and proportions. Place it clearly without covering event information.
- Event-specific artwork and seasonal decorations may remain; they are not substitutes for the Club mark.
- When updating existing flyers, preserve event names, dates, times, prices, reservation deadlines, contact details and approved copy. Check the finished artwork before publication.

Approved October 25, 2026 replacements retain the existing event artwork and use the gold monogram in the branding position.
